# Issue #23 — Supernatural emergence

Creative Director approved the qualitative direction in discussion. Numerical rules and implementation remain open. #6 supplies the remaining entity behavior/counterplay details; #7 supplies outcome evaluation.

## Alternatives
- Energy-only deterministic threshold: sustained fear crosses a threshold and spawns an entity. Simple but predictable and insensitive to beliefs and history.
- Combined probabilistic model (approved): emotional intensity contributes energy, belief facilitates emergence, imprints shape eligible outcomes, and bounded randomness determines whether an entity emerges. No guaranteed spawn, fixed progression stages, or producer selection of entity.

## Approved rules
- The producer creates conditions for emergence, not entities directly. House-wide conditions and localized concentrations coexist; environment, contestants, history and chance shape entities.
- Sustained emotional states and acute significant experiences contribute psychic energy. Fear is not the only contributor. Belief facilitates emergence; skeptical frightened contestants can still contribute.
- Energy is a catalyst, not a consumable resource. It can dissipate as conditions change, but manifestations never spend it or weaken an entity merely through repetition.
- Imprints preserve the qualitative influence of experiences, including locations, objects, people and relationships, beyond immediate emotional states.
- Anchoring supports persistence independently of original causes. Repetition, significant events and attachments are possible routes. Dormant and continuously active persistent hauntings both qualify; distinguishing/scoring them is deferred.
- Every genuine supernatural event is caused by an entity already present. No independent residual phenomenon or pre-entity supernatural event system. Repetitive phenomena can be represented by limited-awareness entities.
- Entities are autonomous participants analogous to contestants, with different capabilities and constraints, not random scare generators. Their actions use the same world-event/perception/appraisal pipeline as practical effects; cause remains private unless observable.
- A deceased contestant may become a ghost retaining aspects of identity, memories, relationships and motivations. Death is neither necessary nor sufficient. No mandatory death quota.
- No exact psychic meters or emergence announcements; ordinary observation rules apply. No elaborate occult research or exposition required.
- Prevention reduces conditions for new emergence; suppression limits existing activity; resolution may remove an entity or its anchor. Exact countermeasures belong to #6.

## Paper scenarios
1. Sustained fear and growing belief increase emergence odds; a seeded run may produce a limited entity that moves an object, while another run produces none. The first genuine disturbance implies an existing entity.
2. Betrayal and a death leave a distinct imprint; a ghost may emerge with related motivations, but is not guaranteed. A nonfatal season can also produce an enduring entity.
3. Excessive fear causes a contestant to quit, reducing future emotional input; more fear is not automatically better.
4. An autonomous entity frightens a contestant the producer wanted to retain; its actions cost no psychic energy and may generate further emotional responses.
5. Production stops: a weakly anchored presence may fail to persist despite high prior activity; a sufficiently anchored presence may endure even as emotional input disappears.

## Minimal implementation candidate
One house, one possible autonomous entity, a small set of existing world-event actions, simple seeded emergence probability informed by emotional conditions and belief, minimal contextual imprint and persistence representation. Demonstrate both emergence and non-emergence, independent action, non-consumption, and persistence distinct from activity. Exact entity behavior and countermeasure await #6; season outcome criteria await #7. No production implementation in this PR.

Defer multiple concurrent entities, elaborate ghost inheritance, rich imprint graphs, artifact systems, detailed spatial energy propagation, entity taxonomies, occult research, dormancy distinctions and scoring refinements until a playable core exists. Preserve the design direction without building speculative abstractions. Tune rates, thresholds and probabilities through playtesting.
