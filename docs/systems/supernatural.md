# Supernatural Entities and Contestant Resistance

## Core premise [CONFIRMED]
The production begins by fabricating haunting effects, but psychic energy produces genuine supernatural activity. With progression, manifestations can act autonomously and pursue goals that do not align with the producer's need to retain contestants or preserve secrecy.

## Three activity classes [PROPOSED]
1. **Practical effects:** player-controlled, logistical costs, detectable mechanisms.
2. **Influenced manifestations:** player can attempt to direct, with uncertain outcomes.
3. **Autonomous entities:** select actions according to drives, attachments, constraints and local state.

## Entity model [PROPOSED]
Traits such as aggression, territoriality, fixation, volatility, responsiveness and independence. A drive/goal selection mechanism should produce legible but not perfectly predictable behavior. Examples are poltergeist, apparition and place-bound spirit; taxonomy not final.

## Contestant counterplay [CONFIRMED]
Contestants can detect and avoid supernatural threats, gather information, develop protections, attempt exorcism/banishment, and sometimes permanently neutralize entities. Cooperative investigation and rituals should be possible in the simulation, not just authored cutscenes.

## Producer conflict [PROPOSED]
Producer may need to preserve an energy-generating entity from contestant banishment, redirect a dangerous manifestation, or prevent autonomous escalation from causing mass departures. An unsuccessful ritual might change the situation, but this is not yet an approved rule.

## Testing contracts
- Entities act without player commands in seeded scenarios.
- Same initial seed and actions reproduce event outcomes.
- Entity choices can be traced to goals/constraints for QA.
- Contestant countermeasures can succeed under defined conditions.
- Entity can disrupt a carefully planned production strategy.
- Entities are not required to behave strategically on behalf of the producer.

## Open decisions
Origins of entity drives; how psychic energy is harvested; degrees of influence/control; entity creation and extinction; ritual mechanics; whether house itself becomes an agent; thresholds and costs.
