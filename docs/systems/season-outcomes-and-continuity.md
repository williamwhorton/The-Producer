# Operation outcomes and continuity

## Approved direction (#7)
The sole victory condition is a genuine persistent haunting: at least one entity remains sufficiently anchored after production ends. Otherwise the season fails. Activity or energy alone is insufficient. Death is neither required nor sufficient; no survivor or body-count gate.

The competition is a lure for a villainous conspiracy. The producer is assumed to evade detection afterward and can repeat the scheme at another property regardless of outcome. Exposure, suspicion, departures and deaths may affect contestants and the current operation, but are not separate victory conditions or campaign-ending failures. No reputation, law-enforcement, cover-up or operational-viability subsystem.

A fixed competition duration ends the season, or it ends early when no living contestants remain in the house. Exact duration, anchoring test and voluntary early termination remain open. Dormant and active persistent entities both qualify.

## Contestant fates
Distinguish voluntary departure, attempted escape, rule-related removal, death, disappearance and survival as applicable. Exact removal mechanics and narrative details remain open. Actual fate and observed knowledge are distinct. The original story's specific prize, duration and witness outcomes remain illustrative, not mandates.

## Verification
- Persistent anchored entity after interventions cease: success, including a dormant entity.
- Intense temporary activity without persistence: failure.
- No deaths or all contestants dead: neither determines success independently.
- Discovery of deception can disrupt the current attempt but does not block a subsequent operation.
- Fixed duration or absence of living occupants terminates the season.
- Subsequent operation can begin without inherited artifacts.

Detailed scoring and narrative consequences are deferred until a playable game exists.
